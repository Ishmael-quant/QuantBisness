About QuantBisness

We help African entrepreneurs discover, evaluate, and launch high-potential business ideas — matched to their budget, region, and goals.

Our Mission

Current economic research shows that 80% of African businesses are born out of survival necessity — leading to severe "copycat" market saturation and high failure rates driven by a lack of localized market data.

QuantBisness was built to solve this structural mismatch. Instead of blind imitation, we use an algorithmic framework that cross-references an entrepreneur's exact Capital, Location, and Industry potential — turning blind survival risks into data-backed business opportunities.

Whether you have 500 shillings or 100,000+, there is a path forward built on evidence, not guesswork. Our goal is to make sure you find it.
