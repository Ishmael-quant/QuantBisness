Q

Our purpose

Make the first step
feel more possible.

Finding a business idea can be overwhelming. It’s hard to know where to begin, what fits your budget, or which opportunities make sense for your community.

QuantBisness brings ideas together in one place and lets you explore them by budget, sector, and region. We want useful inspiration to be easier to find for aspiring entrepreneurs across the continent.

How it works

A simple place to start.

Explore possibilities at your own pace. The goal is to help you move from a broad interest to an idea worth learning more about.

01 ⌕

Discover

Browse business ideas across a range of sectors and find something that sparks your interest.

02 ▽

Explore

Use the available filters to narrow ideas by budget, industry, and one of Kenya’s eight former provinces.

03 ↗

Take a next step

Learn more about an opportunity, ask questions, and decide what to investigate further.

Ideas are a starting point, not a guarantee of success. Research local demand, costs, and requirements before investing.
